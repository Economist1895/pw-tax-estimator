Child Relief (QCR / Disability)
$0
▼
QCR ($4,000) — all three conditions must apply: child is unmarried; child is under 16, or 16+ and studying full-time; child’s annual income is less than $8,000.
Child Relief (Disability) ($7,500) — child is unmarried and physically disabled or mentally impaired (any age, any income).
Annual income includes NS pay/allowances, internship/attachment income, foreign-sourced income, and tax-exempt income (e.g. bank interest), but excludes scholarships and bursaries. Full eligibility rules.

Working Mother’s Child Relief (WMCR)
$0
▼
For working mothers with a Singapore Citizen child who qualifies for QCR / Child Relief (Disability). Automatically computed from your children below. For each Singapore Citizen child: 1st = $8,000 / 15% of earned income; 2nd = $10,000 / 20%; 3rd+ = $12,000 / 25% (fixed amount if born from 2024; % of earned income if born before). Combined QCR + WMCR is capped at $50,000 per child. Birth order is auto-counted from your list of children. If you have earlier natural-born children not on the form (stillborn, deceased, or older non-eligible), set the "Natural birth order" field on the affected child — per IRAS, all natural-born children count toward WMCR birth order. Full WMCR rules.
